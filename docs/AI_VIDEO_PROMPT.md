## PROMPT (copy from here)

Create a professional 3-minute startup pitch video for **RAFIQ**, an AI-powered Accessibility Marketplace (Android app) that connects people with disabilities to trained support assistants, with on-device sign-language recognition, AI chat, accessible maps, emergency SOS and medication reminders.

**Style:** 16:9, clean modern tech startup. Dark-navy background (#0B1626) with cyan (#03DCE1) and blue (#0155F3) accents. Confident, warm, human tone — accessibility tech, not "charity". Subtle motion: animated cards, numbers counting up, app screens sliding in. No stock sad-music; use hopeful/upbeat corporate music. English narration. Captions always ON.

**Brand facts to show:** name spelled RAFIQ (Arabic رفيق, means "companion/friend"). Tagline: "Trained human assistance, matched by AI." App screens: Assistant Booking, AI Matching results, Sign Language gesture view, Accessible Map, SOS, Medication reminders.

**Structure (8 scenes):**

1. **Hook (0:00–0:15):** Animated stat: "1 in 6 people worldwide lives with a disability (WHO — ~1.3 billion)." Narration: "For them, daily life is a maze — and trained help on demand barely exists."
2. **Problem (0:15–0:45):** Icons splitting: map app / reminder app / SOS app — all separate. Narration: "The tools exist, but they're fragmented. A map here, a reminder there, and no way to book a trained, trusted assistant. So most people fall back on family, or on strangers."
3. **Solution (0:45–1:10):** RAFIQ logo reveals, Arabic رفيق subtitle. Narration: "RAFIQ is one companion that ties it together — an AI-powered accessibility marketplace." Show app Home screen.
4. **The Marketplace (1:10–1:40):** Book flow: pick need (wheelchair/sign language/vision/elderly) → set from/to + budget → AI-matched assistants ranked → confirm with estimated cost. Narration: "Pick your need, your journey and your budget. RAFIQ's matching AI scores every assistant on skill match, rating, distance and price — so you book trained help with full transparency, in under two minutes."
5. **The AI Suite (1:40–2:15):** Show sign-language gesture → text on screen (offline); then voice AI chat; then accessible map with wheelchair/sign/braille places; then SOS countdown. Narration: "Around it: real-time sign language that runs on-device — private, with zero internet. An AI that speaks back. An accessible map. And an SOS that calls for help in ten seconds."
6. **Impact + Jobs (2:15–2:40):** KPI cards count up: "Book in <2 min", "18/18 tests passing", "Jobs for trained assistants", "Aligned with SDG 3, 8, 10, 11". Narration: "RAFIQ creates a skills economy — independence for users, income and dignity for trained assistants. Accessible by design, offline by default."
7. **Business (2:40–2:55):** Revenue icons: commission, premium plans, hospitals/NGOS/corporates, AI services. Narration: "A two-sided marketplace with ten revenue streams — commission on bookings, premium subscriptions, and B2B accessibility contracts."
8. **Close (2:55–3:00):** RAFIQ logo + tagline. Narration: "Accessibility isn't a feature. It's the whole product. RAFIQ — your companion, every step." Fade to logo + URL github.com/seifmomo/Rafiq.

**AI video instructions:** keep every scene ≤ 20 seconds; one main overlaid animated number/visual per scene; avoid text walls (max ~8 words of on-screen text per scene, captions separate); use a single warm presenter or directed motion-graphics style (pick one); end card holds 3 seconds.
